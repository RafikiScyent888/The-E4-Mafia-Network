          SO-J4 preview · The IPAM sheet: subnet the office by hand

          The office has 192.168.40.0/24. Plan one block for each group, each only as big as it needs, none overlapping, and write every value yourself. The sheet checks what you wrote and says what's wrong, never what the answer is.

          Terminal · Office switch (console)

          This is the switch's own console: the laptop's serial terminal on the console cable, the way a new switch is first set up. Press Enter to run a line, Tab to finish a word, ? for what can come next. Any unique start of a word works (conf t, sh run).

          Start with ?, then enable. Nothing you type here is saved after you leave this preview.

          How the small office works

          The second level. You set up the office's first managed switch from its console, then from the office PC over SSH, you configure its ports and you plan the office's addresses by hand. Tickets break those same things for you to find with the show commands.

          1. Finish a level to open the next. A level is finished when every one of its tickets and work orders is done: that's every objective it teaches. Your instructor's PIN opens every level.
          2. Show before configure. Look with the show commands, change with the configuration ones, then show again to prove it.
          3. Save it. Only what you save survives a reload or a power cut.
