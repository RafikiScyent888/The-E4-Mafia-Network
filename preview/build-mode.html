J3 · The porch camera

Work order
22 Maple Street

Mr. Okafor · 22 Maple Street · new house, the internet just put in

“The boxes are all here. I want the camera on the porch working, and I want to see it on my phone when I'm away. The printer has to work from the office computer every time, and my son's room upstairs never gets any Wi-Fi.”

Mason: Read the whole order before you unbox anything. Plan the runs, then set things up.

Limits on this job

  • A copper run is 100 m at most: each run's length shows as you lay it.
  • The camera draws up to 6.5 W over its cable; an 802.3af PoE injector gives up to 15.4 W.
  • No budget limit: use what the job needs.

In the box, not placed yet

  • IP camera
  • PoE injector (802.3af)
  • Mesh node
